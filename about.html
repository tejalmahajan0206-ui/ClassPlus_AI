PROJECT GUIDE

What ClassPulse AI does

A student-friendly prototype for a multimodal attendance and activity dashboard.

▦

Attendance dashboard

Daily roster, present/absent/unverified statuses, manual correction, and CSV export.

◉

Face-assisted matching

Opt-in enrollment and local OpenCV LBPH matching. A teacher must verify the suggested identity before marking present.

Status: {{ 'Dependencies available' if cv_available else 'Install opencv-contrib-python to enable' }}

🎙

Voice-assisted lookup

Uses the browser's Web Speech API to transcribe a spoken name/code, then asks the teacher to select and confirm the student.

↗

Activity analytics

Records participation, quiz score and poll responses, then shows a transparent average and exports data to CSV.

Privacy, safety & known limitations

  • Use only with informed, explicit consent and follow your institution's rules.
  • Face samples stay on this computer under data/faces/. Disable consent to delete a student's samples, or delete the project data folder to remove all face data.
  • Face recognition can make mistakes due to lighting, pose, camera quality, and similar appearances. It is a suggestion, not proof of identity.
  • Voice recognition depends on browser support and its speech service. The app does not save audio recordings.
  • Manual attendance is always available. Do not use face/voice matching as the sole attendance method.
  • Activity score is based only on entered participation, quiz and poll values. No emotion, attention, or learning state is inferred from a face or voice.
  • This is a local educational prototype, not a production-ready secure school system. It has no login/roles yet; do not expose it to the public internet.
